
Let $\mathcal{I} = \{1,2,\dots,P\}$ denote a team of $P$ agents. Agent $i=1$ is the primary agent tasked with reaching a goal location $G \in \mathbb{R}^2$. All agents move at constant speed $v_i$, making minimization of time equivalent to minimizing trajectory length.

\subsection{Decision Variables}

The optimization is performed over mean-state trajectories. Specifically, the decision variables are the heading sequences
\[
\{\theta_i[k]\}_{i \in \mathcal{I},\, k=0}^{N-1}.
\]

Positions are deterministically induced by these controls:
\[
x_i[k+1] = x_i[k] + v_i \Delta t
\begin{bmatrix}
\cos \theta_i[k] \\
\sin \theta_i[k]
\end{bmatrix}.
\]

\subsection{Covariance Propagation}

Let $\Sigma_i[k]$ denote the covariance of agent $i$. Covariance is not a decision variable and is propagated deterministically for constraint evaluation.

We assume linearized motion with anisotropic process noise:
\[
\Sigma_i[k+1|k] = F(\theta_i[k])\,\Sigma_i[k|k-1]\,F(\theta_i[k])^\top + Q_i[k],
\]
where
\[
Q_i[k] =
\begin{bmatrix}
\sigma^2_{\parallel}[k] + \alpha_{\parallel}\Delta t & 0 \\
0 & \sigma^2_{\perp}[k] + \alpha_{\perp}\Delta t
\end{bmatrix}.
\]

\subsection{Measurement Model (Tapered Gaussian Sensing)}

When an agent is within sensing range of a landmark or another agent, a measurement update is applied. The Kalman gain is
\[
K_i[k] = \Sigma_i[k|k-1] H^\top \left(H \Sigma_i[k|k-1] H^\top + R\right)^{-1},
\]
and the covariance is updated using the Joseph form:
\[
\Sigma_i[k] = (I - K_i[k]H)\Sigma_i[k|k-1](I - K_i[k]H)^\top + K_i[k] R K_i[k]^\top.
\]

Sensing quality is modeled via a tapered Gaussian function of distance:
\[
w_{ij}(k) = \exp\left(-\frac{\|x_i[k]-x_j[k]\|^2}{2\sigma_c^2}\right),
\]
which modulates measurement availability.

\subsection{Constraints}

\textbf{Terminal uncertainty constraint:}
\[
\det(\Sigma_i[N]) \le \bar{d}, \quad \forall i \in \mathcal{I}.
\]
Here, $\bar{d}$ is the maximum allowable terminal covariance determinant.

\textbf{Dynamics:}
\[
x_i[k+1] = x_i[k] + v_i \Delta t
\begin{bmatrix}
\cos \theta_i[k] \\
\sin \theta_i[k]
\end{bmatrix}.
\]

\textbf{Curvature constraint:}
\[
|\theta_i[k+1] - \theta_i[k]| \le \frac{v_i \Delta t}{r}.
\]

\textbf{Initial conditions:}
\[
x_i[0] = x_i^0, \qquad \Sigma_i[0] = \Sigma_i^0, \quad \forall i \in \mathcal{I}.
\]

\subsection{Optimization Problem}

\[
\min_{\{\theta_i[k]\},\,N} \quad N
\]
subject to the above constraints.
